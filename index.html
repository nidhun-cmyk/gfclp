Who needs to file corporate tax

If you fall into any of these categories, a return is due, regardless of whether tax is actually owed.

Mainland

Every UAE mainland company must register and file, regardless of profit.

Free Zone

Even at a 0% rate, free zone entities must file to keep QFZP status.

Foreign Branch

UAE branches of foreign companies are taxable persons and must file.

Sole Establishment

Individuals with over AED 1M in UAE business turnover are also in scope.
